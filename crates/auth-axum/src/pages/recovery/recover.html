Experimental reference client; independent policy review and device evidence are still required. No mailbox/password fallback or automatic identity lookup is available.

A code is reserved once for this browser. Keep this browser and its cookies for the whole wait. A lost cookie, cancelled request or expired request may require a working key to configure new proof. Your existing keys stay usable during the wait.

The minimum wait is 24 hours and the request expires after seven days. With a verified contact, the wait begins only after SMTP acceptance, not proof you read the alert. Without a verified contact there is no independent email alert. A prolonged delivery outage can prevent completion before expiry.

Do this explicitly if a session cookie exists, including an expired one. Recovery never silently switches your signed-in account.
