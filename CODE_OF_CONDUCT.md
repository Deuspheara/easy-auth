# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and experience
levels. Critique designs and code, explain concerns clearly, and protect personal
information. This policy applies to repository discussions and project events.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and sustained
disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation
or exclude participants for violations, with proportional action and an opportunity
to clarify where safe.

## Reporting concerns

A dedicated verified private conduct-reporting contact is not yet published and
must be established before community launch. Do not post sensitive personal
details in public reports. The [security policy](SECURITY.md) covers vulnerability
reports separately.
