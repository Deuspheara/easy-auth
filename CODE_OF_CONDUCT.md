# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique designs and code, explain concerns clearly, and protect personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or exclude participants for violations, with proportional action and an opportunity to clarify where safe.

A verified private maintainer contact must be added before community launch. Until then, do not post sensitive personal details in public reports. This policy applies to repository discussions and project events.
